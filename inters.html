Celestial Constellations

Ancient patterns written in starlight — each one a chapter in humanity's oldest storybook.

Mythology
Orion, the Hunter

The mighty hunter stands eternal in the winter sky. His three-star belt has guided sailors and dreamers for ten thousand years.

Queen of Aethiopia
Cassiopeia's Crown

Shaped like a W in the northern sky, the vain queen Cassiopeia reigns eternally — circling the pole without ever setting below the horizon.

Navigation
Ursa Major — The Great Bear

Home to the famous Big Dipper, Ursa Major has pointed generations of travellers toward the North Star, whispering the way home.

Summer Sky
Scorpius & Antares

The great scorpion blazes in the summer sky, its heart marked by the red supergiant Antares — a star 700 times the size of our sun.

Music of the Spheres
Lyra & Vega

Orpheus's lyre, placed among the stars by Zeus. Its brightest jewel, Vega, will become Earth's north star in 14,000 years.

Galaxy
Andromeda & Her Galaxy

Chained to the rocks yet carrying an entire galaxy within her borders — our nearest cosmic neighbour, visible to the naked eye.

✦ The Cosmos

Beyond the
Observable

The universe is 93 billion light-years wide — and growing. It is 13.8 billion years old, made almost entirely of things we cannot see, cannot touch, and have only begun to imagine.

There are more stars in the universe than grains of sand on every beach on Earth.
95% of the universe is dark matter and dark energy — invisible forces holding everything together.
Every atom in your body was forged inside a dying star. You are, literally, made of the cosmos.
The universe has no centre — every point in space sees every other point moving away from it.
